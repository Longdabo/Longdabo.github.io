HideMaster Privacy Policy

This policy explains how X App Team handles information in HideMaster for Android (com.xapp.waveleap.hider).

Information handled on your device

  • HideMaster uses the installed-app list to show you which apps you can hide, group or lock. If you enable App Lock, Android usage access helps HideMaster detect when a selected app opens. Display-over-other-apps access lets HideMaster show the unlock screen.
  • HideMaster accesses the files or media you select so it can hide them, import them to your vault, or restore them. Vault items and private notes are stored on your device using encryption. If you create a backup or export, it goes to the location you select. Android backup settings can also affect the retention of eligible app data.
  • If you enable break-in alerts and grant camera access, HideMaster can take and save a photo on the device after failed unlock attempts. If you enable notification shielding, HideMaster reads the notification information needed to identify notifications from selected apps and dismiss them.
  • Android handles biometric verification when it is available and enabled. HideMaster receives the result of the verification.
  • The browser stores browser data such as bookmarks, history, cookies and downloads on your device. Sites and search engines you visit receive requests from your device and apply their own privacy policies. If you grant a website camera, microphone or location access, it can use that permission. Download and filter-list requests go to the selected or configured provider.

Advertising, analytics and crash reporting

HideMaster includes Google Mobile Ads and may use Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Unity Ads and Mintegral for ad mediation. These services can receive your IP address and approximate location derived from it, advertising and other device or installation identifiers, device and app information, interactions with ads or the app, and performance or diagnostic information. The services use this information to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and detect fraud. Data use can vary with your device, privacy choices and the ad provider involved.

Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics can receive installation identifiers, app and device information, usage events, session information and crash reports. These services help us understand app usage, diagnose errors and improve reliability. You can change the crash reporting setting in HideMaster.

For information on how providers process data and handle privacy requests, see Google, Meta, AppLovin, Unity and Mintegral.

Your choices, retention and deletion

You can manage Android permissions and the Android advertising ID in your device settings. HideMaster does not require a HideMaster account. You can clear browser data and delete vault items or break-in photos from the app. Clearing Android app storage deletes local app data. Copies you exported or that are held in a backup must be managed where you saved them.

Advertising and diagnostic providers retain information according to their services and policies. You can use the contact below to ask about access or deletion of information we handle. The providers above offer their own privacy request channels for information they control.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact X App Team using the support email on the HideMaster Google Play listing. We will post updates to this policy on this page.
